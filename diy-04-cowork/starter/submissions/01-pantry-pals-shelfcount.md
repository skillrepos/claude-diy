## What it does

Eastside Community Food Bank logs every donation by hand on paper, then a coordinator types it into a spreadsheet on Monday. Nobody knows what is running low until the shelves are empty.

ShelfCount lets volunteers log donations by pointing a phone camera at the barcode. A dashboard shows stock by category, highlights anything below a minimum the coordinator sets, and sends the coordinator a short email every Friday with the five most urgent items to ask donors for.

We tested it with two volunteers from the food bank on Sunday morning: they logged 140 items in 25 minutes. The demo video shows the whole flow on a real phone.
