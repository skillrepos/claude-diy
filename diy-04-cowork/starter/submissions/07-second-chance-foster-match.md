## What it does

Riverbend Animal Rescue has more animals than foster homes, and staff match fosters to pets from memory and a stack of paper forms.

Foster Match gives fosters an online form (yard, other pets, allergies, hours at home) and suggests the best three pets for each foster, explaining why. Staff approve every match before anyone is contacted.

Rescue staff reviewed the screenshots on Sunday and want to try it with their next intake.
