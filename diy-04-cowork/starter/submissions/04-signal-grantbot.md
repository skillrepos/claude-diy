## What it does

The Riverbend Arts Council applies for about 25 grants a year and missed two deadlines last year, losing an estimated $18,000 in funding.

GrantBot reads the council's grant calendar and posts reminders in their team chat at 30, 14 and 3 days before each deadline, with a checklist of the documents each funder asks for. When a document is uploaded to the shared drive, it ticks that item off.

The council's director watched the demo and said it would have saved both missed grants. Sorry this is a bit late — our wifi died right before noon.
