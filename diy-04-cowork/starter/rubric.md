# How we judge submissions

Score each submission 1–5 on each of these. Higher is better.

1. **Impact** — does it solve a real problem for a named nonprofit?
2. **It works** — is there evidence it runs (a demo video, screenshots, a live link)?
3. **Clear** — can a judge quickly tell what it does and who it's for?
4. **Original** — a fresh idea, or a fresh angle on a familiar one?

**Total** = sum of the four (max 20).

## Not eligible for Demo Night
Score these anyway, but they can't be picked:
- Submitted after the deadline in event.md.
- The same project entered more than once (keep the earliest entry and note the duplicate).
- No link to the code.

## Ties
Prefer the team where everyone is at their first hackathon.
